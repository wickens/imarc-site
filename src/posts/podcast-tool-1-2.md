I have updated _Podcast Tool_ – a lightweight program for managing podcast subscriptions. The major new feature is it now includes playback functionality. I have also made many optimisations, so working with shows that have hundreds of episodes no longer causes a slowdown. The reason for adding playback functionality is so I can keep track of the playback position, meaning you can pause a podcast, close the program and when you come back to it, Podcast Tool will resume from where you left off.

### Main Screen

Shows are listed along the left side of the screen, with individual episodes on the right.
